A LITTLE HELP

Beta questions, answered.

Why do I need to join the Google Group?

Google Play uses the group to give testers access to this beta. Join with your Google Play account, then join the beta on the testing page. Filling out the form does not add you to the group.

Why are there two Google Play links?

The testing page lets you join the beta. The Play Store page lets you install or open the app after joining. Open the testing page first.

What if the Play beta page says I don’t have access yet?

Check that you joined the Google Group and that Google Play is using the same account. Google Play may need a moment to see that you joined. Wait a moment, open the testing page again and try once more. If you’re still stuck, contact support.

Do I need Android?

Yes. This beta is only for Android. Install SnipStik on your Android phone through Google Play.

Do I need WhatsApp installed?

You can make stickers without it. To use them in WhatsApp, install WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business.

How do I give feedback?

Follow the beta instructions, or email servinsystems@gmail.com. Tell us what you tried, your device model and what worked or went wrong. No positive review or public rating is required.

What is the reward for founding testers?

The first 30 eligible testers who complete all requirements will receive SnipStik Pro Lifetime free when Pro launches. Joining alone does not guarantee the reward. Read the full eligibility details.
