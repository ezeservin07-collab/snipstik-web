Privacy

This page explains how the current SnipStik Android app and its beta signup handle information.

Sticker creation stays on your device

SnipStik performs sticker processing locally on your Android device. The app currently has no accounts, cloud storage or analytics.

This website does not use analytics, trackers or cookies.

Beta signup information

Beta signup is handled using Google Forms. The form may collect:

  • Your Google Play email.
  • Android device and model information.
  • Your country, if you voluntarily supply it.
  • Beta participation responses.
  • Your feedback.

Servin Systems uses this information only to administer the beta, contact testers and determine Founding Tester reward eligibility. Tester information is not sold.

Google Forms

Google Forms and Google services may process submitted form data under Google’s own privacy practices. You leave this website when you open the signup form.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy.

Contact and deletion requests

You may request deletion of your beta signup information or ask a privacy question by contacting Servin Systems at servinsystems@gmail.com.
